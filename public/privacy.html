Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-20

opnjrnl ("we", "our", or "us") operates as an offline-first journal application.

Data Collection and Usage

All your journal data, including written entries, moods, and tags, are stored strictly on your local device (within your browser's Local Storage). We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell any of your personal data to remote servers.

Third-Party Services

If you choose to sync your data using third-party services like Google Drive through the app interface, your synced data is securely transferred and stored under the privacy policies and security protocols of Google Drive respectively. We do not have separate access to your synced data.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the associated source code repository.
